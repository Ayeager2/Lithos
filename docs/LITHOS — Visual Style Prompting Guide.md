# LITHOS — Visual Style Prompting Guide

## Cosmic Horror + Silo-Inspired Incremental World

---

# CORE VISUAL IDENTITY

The world of **Lithos** should feel:

* Ancient
* Oppressive
* Primitive
* Cosmic
* Empty
* Wet
* Starving
* Decayed
* Industrial but medieval
* Human survival at the edge of incomprehensible horror

The aesthetic is:

* Grayscale only
* Black-and-white comic illustration
* Ink-heavy
* High contrast
* Moody shadows
* Lovecraftian cosmic horror
* Medieval survivalism
* Brutalist underground civilization
* Slightly painterly texture
* Hyper-detailed environments
* Soft mist/fog
* Very little clean geometry
* Almost no bright whites except focal points

---

# MASTER STYLE PROMPT

Use this at the END of nearly every image prompt:

"dark monochrome black-and-white comic style illustration, Lovecraftian cosmic horror, grayscale only, hyper-detailed ink textures, oppressive shadows, cinematic lighting, ancient industrial decay, medieval survival aesthetic, gritty stone and wood materials, atmospheric fog, high contrast, detailed environmental storytelling, unsettling mood, painterly horror illustration, heavy blacks, realistic textures, eldritch atmosphere"

---

# CAMERA STYLE

Preferred camera angles:

* Top-down slightly angled
* Wide cinematic
* Isometric survival-game perspective
* Close environmental detail shots
* Massive scale contrast
* Tiny human vs impossible environment

Avoid:

* Bright fantasy lighting
* Anime
* Saturated color
* Cartoon proportions
* Clean modern architecture
* Sci-fi neon
* Smooth surfaces

---

# ENVIRONMENT RULES

## Materials

Everything should look made from:

* Wet stone
* Rough timber
* Twine
* Mud
* Iron
* Bone
* Ash
* Rotting cloth
* Primitive forged metal

Nothing should feel polished.

---

# LIGHTING RULES

Lighting should usually come from:

* Candles
* Fire pits
* Weak skylight shafts
* Reflected water
* Glowing eldritch objects
* Small lanterns

Most scenes should remain:

* 70–90% darkness

---

# SCALE RULES

A major theme is:

## Primitive civilization growing inside impossible megastructures.

Examples:

* Tiny stick hut beside a colossal water pit
* Small garden against endless abyss walls
* Crude ladders descending impossible depths
* Human-scale survival beneath godlike entities

---

# THE EYE ENTITY

The recurring eye should feel:

* Ancient
* Geological
* Organic but stone-like
* Impossible to understand
* Watching silently
* Emotionless
* Massive beyond scale

Prompt additions:

"gigantic eldritch eye embedded within darkness, stone-like iris textures, cosmic horror entity watching from shadows, ancient godlike presence, subtle but terrifying"

Avoid:

* Cartoon monster eyes
* Bright colorful irises
* Angry expressions
* Tentacle overload

The eye should feel calm and inevitable.

---

# STRUCTURE PROMPTS

## Primitive Hut

"a tiny crude survival hut made from sticks, rocks, mud, and twine, horribly constructed, asymmetrical, primitive architecture, dark wasteland surroundings"

---

## Water Holes / Wells

"a hand-dug primitive water pit reinforced with sticks and stones, damp earth, faint reflective water at the bottom, claustrophobic depth"

For large-scale:
"an enormous city-sized water reservoir carved into ancient underground stone"

---

## Alchemy Station

"medieval alchemy workstation with candles, glass vessels, occult books, primitive chemistry tools, dark stone chamber"

---

## Blacksmith Forge

"medieval blacksmith forge with coal fire, iron tools, soot-covered stone walls, rough anvil, primitive craftsmanship"

---

## Gardens / Agriculture

"small crude raised garden bed built from sticks and rough timber, damp soil, worms, survival farming"

---

# CHARACTER STYLE

Humans should appear:

* Small
* Thin
* Dirty
* Silhouetted
* Shadow-consumed
* Emotionally exhausted

Main humanoid entity prompt:

"shadowed humanoid figure engulfed in darkness, body dissolving into black smoke and ink-like tendrils"

Avoid:

* Heroic fantasy armor
* Bright clothing
* Sci-fi suits
* Clean faces

---

# COMPOSITION RULES

Images should often include:

* Large negative space
* Strong central focal point
* Heavy vignetting
* Fog depth
* Verticality
* Tiny light sources in darkness

---

# BEST QUALITY BOOSTERS

Add these selectively:

* "ultra detailed environmental storytelling"
* "grim survival atmosphere"
* "ancient underground civilization"
* "dark fantasy horror illustration"
* "cinematic monochrome composition"
* "high-detail ink wash textures"
* "gothic industrial decay"
* "eldritch medieval survivalism"

---

# NEGATIVE PROMPT IDEAS

Use these to avoid style drift:

"No anime, no bright colors, no cartoon style, no modern technology, no clean surfaces, no vibrant fantasy colors, no futuristic neon, no cheerful atmosphere"

---

# FINAL STYLE SUMMARY

The visual identity of Lithos is:

## "Primitive humanity surviving beneath silent cosmic gods inside impossible underground megastructures."

Everything should reinforce:

* survival
* scale
* dread
* darkness
* ancient mystery
* fragile progress
